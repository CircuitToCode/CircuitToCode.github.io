Smart EV Charging Timer.

An embedded-control concept for scheduled electric-vehicle charging and timed power management.

← Back to project lab

01 / OVERVIEW

Project overview

This concept explores how a microcontroller-based controller can manage a charging schedule, provide configurable timing and support safer, more convenient energy use.

Engineering contribution

  • Exploring a timer-based charging-control workflow.
  • Considering microcontroller inputs, outputs and user configuration.
  • Planning relay control, status indication and fail-safe behaviour.

Technical focus

Microcontroller firmware, timer logic, input/output control and electrical safety considerations.

Key learning

Embedded products must combine reliable firmware, electrical safety and clear user feedback.
